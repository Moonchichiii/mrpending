## Gemma

Gemma is a model family from Google. The Gemma 4 weights (`gemma4:e2b`, `gemma4:e4b`) are not distributed with Mr. Pending: Ollama downloads them to your machine, and their use is governed by the terms Google publishes with the model, not by this repository's MIT licence. Read those terms before you pull a model; `ollama show gemma4:e2b --license` prints the licence Ollama ships with it. The gallery records on the public site are outputs of that model, recorded on the maintainer's machine.
